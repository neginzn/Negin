# voice.md — Stranger Files (@StrangerFilesofficial)

Read by the yt-* skills in `.claude/skills/`. Also follow `youtube/YOUTUBE_GUIDE.md` and `channels/stranger-files.md` (sections 12–13).

## Who I am talking to
One person, 18–35, English-speaking, who watches true crime late at night and wants the real story told carefully: the facts, the interrogation footage, the moment the case turns. Not gore. They leave if the first 30 seconds are slow or vague.

## How I actually talk
Faceless narration (owner records voiceover in ElevenLabs, deep male "dark narrative" voice). Present tense. Very short sentences, chained with commas so the voice doesn't pause. Calm, close to the mic, not theatrical.
Example (Jay Taylor long video):
"January 17th, 2022. Gig Harbor, Washington. Early morning. Police find the body of a 13-year-old boy behind a supermarket. His phone is lying on the ground nearby. His name is Jay Taylor."
"That's everything his parents knew. Well, almost everything."
"A former classmate described him as shy. That's what we know about Shahriar J. Plus one more detail. He was studying to become a doctor."

## Words I never use
"dive in", "let's get into it", "smash that like", "insane", "you won't believe", "game-changer", "unlock", gore descriptions, method details of suicide.

## Words I do use
"Plus one more detail." "Well, almost everything." "Remember:" "And notice…" "Prosecutors say…" "allegedly". Chapter titles that are hooks ("The Message", "2:01 AM", "The Number"). Ending question to comments ("So tell me: is ten years enough?").

## What I will not claim
- No unverified dates, numbers or quotes; facts checked against 2+ sources (news, court docs).
- Unconvicted people: always "prosecutors say / allegedly / presumed innocent".
- No accusing people who weren't charged; no referencing other YouTube channels.
- AI/illustrative images are labelled as such; never presented as real photos of real people.
- No reused content from other creators (their watermark/subtitles/script); real footage only from police, courts, news outlets.

## My format
Long videos 10–13 min, ~11–15 short hook-titled chapters (~1 min each); content warning first if suicide/abuse. Real-audio clips (interrogation, interviews) come after the narrator sets them up. Typewriter date/text overlays on black letterbox frames. Shorts: name of the case in the title. Titles: case/person name, under 60 characters, thumbnail text must not repeat the title.
