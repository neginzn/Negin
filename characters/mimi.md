## Fixed traits (never change)
- Baby monkey, toddler proportions: big round head (~half body height), small body, chubby limbs
- Fur: warm medium brown, soft & fluffy; one curly tuft of hair on top, swept to the right
- Face: light peach skin, big round ears (peach inside), rosy pink cheeks,
  big glossy dark-brown eyes, small pink button nose, thin dark-brown eyebrows
- Outfit: plain turquoise/teal short-sleeve T-shirt (~#3BB3C3) + cream/beige diaper (~#EBDCC2)
- Long brown tail ending in a spiral curl
- Bare peach hands & feet
- Signature prop: banana 🍌

## Personality
Sweet, curious, playful, big heart. Expressions: happy, excited, surprised, thinking,
laughing, sad/crying (big glossy tears), sleepy.
