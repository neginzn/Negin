# What If channel — generic toy-brick style (decided 26 Nov 2026)

## Idea
"What if…" explainers (what if it was always winter, one person ruled the world, one global time, robots in charge…). Story-like timeline (Day 1 → Week 1 → Year 1 → Year 100), not a dry lecture. Real science, honest about guesses ("probably").

## Visual style: generic toy bricks (NOT LEGO)
- Glossy plastic building bricks, studs visible, shallow depth of field, tilt-shift miniature feel, bright cheerful light that can turn dark/cold for dramatic moments.
- The world (towns, cities, forests, ice, Earth) is built from generic bricks.
- **Legal safety (must follow):** never write the word LEGO, no LEGO logo or product names, no copy of the official minifigure design. Figures are our own: round or squared block head, simple painted face, **mitten hands (no C-shaped claw hands)**, no stud on top of the head, non-standard proportions, our own outfit colours. Prompts always contain "generic toy bricks, no brand names, no logos".
- No text inside AI images; all text/numbers are motion graphics added in edit.

## Channel identity
- Recurring host figure (to design) who asks the question and reacts.
- Digital HUD top-left shows the changing numbers (temperature, population, year, etc.), same style as the Ray channel.
- Same production pipeline as Ray: Flow images → ElevenLabs voice → Claude edit with motion graphics, SFX, captions.

## Language plan
English first. Persian version later (same footage, Persian voice + Persian on-screen text) once the English channel has ~3 videos.

## Master style line for prompts
`Glossy generic plastic toy bricks with visible studs, tilt-shift miniature look, shallow depth of field, soft studio lighting, handmade toy-model feel, 16:9, no text, no letters, no logos, no brand names, no watermark.`
