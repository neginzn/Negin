## Title (انتخاب)
Jay Taylor: The Boy Who Said No 30 Times (764 Case)

## Description
In January 2022, 13-year-old Jay Taylor from Gig Harbor, Washington, died after a night online. Prosecutors in Hamburg, Germany, say the person running that call was a 17-year-old known as "White Tiger", allegedly part of the 764 network.

Shahriar J., known online as "White Tiger", faces 204 charges in Hamburg, including one count of murder and five of attempted murder, involving more than 30 children and adolescents in several countries. This is the story of Jay, the Australian teenager who saved the evidence, the warnings that were missed, and a German law that caps the sentence at ten years.
