# Episode 3: 30 Days at Home, When the Aliens Attacked the City

Target length: 10 to 12 minutes
Tone: simple, conversational, story-driven and suspenseful

📊 = on-screen counter; not read aloud.
🔊 = sound effect; not read aloud.

(Literal translation of the Persian script `ep03-alien-invasion-30-days.md`. Keep the two files in sync.)

## The Rules of This World

1. The ships are in the sky and the aliens are on the ground.

2. Any human who gets identified: within a few seconds a ship appears above them and that person disappears.

3. The aliens are sensitive to the light, sound and movement of humans in open spaces. If you stay still in darkness and silence, it becomes much harder for them to detect you, but not impossible.

## Characters

You: grey cap, navy hoodie. Calm and calculating.

Jax: your friend who lives on the upper floor. Knit beanie, black tracksuit and a silver chain. Mischievous, carefree and a little too brave!

## Screenplay

**[Hook | 0:00]**

You're sitting at home watching TV.

Suddenly the program cuts off.

The screen turns red and an emergency message appears:

"Warning! Warning! This is an emergency situation!"

A few seconds later, the sound of an explosion comes from outside.

The windows shake.

**[The Attack Begins | 0:15]**

You look out the window.

Above you there are a whole lot of giant ships. Each one as big as a stadium.

They move silently over the city.

And below, in the streets, them.

The aliens.

They're taking people away with them.

The street is full of crashed cars, a building farther down is on fire and people are fleeing in terror.

But the scariest part is still to come.

A girl is running in the middle of the street.

One of the aliens notices her.

A few seconds later, a ship comes right above the girl.

A white light, and the girl... disappears.

In the middle of the street, only her shoes remain.

A pair of red sneakers.

🔊 The sound of an explosion.

Then the TV turns on the danger alarm.

"All citizens, be prepared for the next thirty days and stay in your homes."

Thirty days?

That means you have to survive thirty days in this very house.

Without them finding you.

**[Day 1: Getting Ready | 1:15]**

The first thing you do is calculate.

Water.

A person needs about four liters of water per day for drinking and basic needs.

For thirty days, that's one hundred and twenty liters.

But you only have six bottles of water.

As long as the tap water hasn't been cut off, you fill the bathtub. Then the pots, the bottles and the buckets.

In total, you collect one hundred and eighty liters of water.

Then it's food's turn.

You empty the cabinets.

Rice, pasta, canned food, bread and eggs.

An adult human needs about forty-five to fifty-four thousand calories of food for thirty days, with rationing.

With rationing, your current food is enough for twenty-two days.

But thirty days?

No.

You have to find a way.

📊 Day 1 | 👥 1 Person | 💧 180 Liters | 🥫 22 Portions | 🔋 Power ON

**[Day 3: The First Mistake | 2:00]**

Three days have passed.

The power is still on, but the internet keeps cutting out.

Nobody knows exactly what's happening, and everyone has written about one of the people around them going missing.

Your food isn't enough for thirty days.

So you have to go to the supermarket.

You carefully leave the building.

The street is almost empty.

Abandoned cars, broken glass and things lying on the ground.

Then at the intersection, you see a few people screaming and running away.

Three aliens are behind them.

One of them turns its head.

A blue light moves across the street.

A few seconds later, one of the people disappears.

Then the second person.

You take cover behind a car.

You hold your breath.

The alien turns toward you.

It comes a few steps closer.

You don't even blink.

A few seconds pass.

Then the alien turns its head away and moves away from you.

You go back home.

Without food.

But you figured out one important thing.

They detect your movement; but when you stayed still, they couldn't find you.

Rule one: in open spaces, any unnecessary movement could be your last mistake.

📊 Day 3 | 👥 1 Person | 💧 172 Liters | 🥫 20 Portions

**[Day 5: Darkness | 3:00]**

It's nine at night.

Suddenly the power of the whole city goes out.

From the window, everywhere is black.

Only the light of the ships can be seen, slowly moving over the buildings.

A light stops on the window of the building across from you.

One of the neighbors has lit a candle there.

A few minutes later, two aliens enter their building.

The candlelight was visible from outside like a big sign.

You close the curtains and hang a blanket behind them.

From now on, at night, no light must go out of the window.

The fridge has also shut off.

Perishable foods must be used sooner. Canned food and dry food stay for the coming days.

There's only one problem.

The gas is still working, but you don't know for how long.

📊 Day 5 | 👥 1 Person | 💧 164 Liters | 🥫 18 Portions | 🔋 Power OFF

**[Day 7: Water | 3:45]**

On the morning of the seventh day, the tap starts to drip.

Then it cuts off completely.

The city's pumps don't work without power.

Luckily, the bathtub is still full of water.

But you'll remember one thing: water stored for washing is not necessarily safe to drink. Boiling kills some contaminants, but not all chemicals.

So for drinking water, you use your clean bottles first.

You keep the bathtub water for washing and the toilet.

For the toilet, there's also a simple way.

If you pour a bucket of water into the toilet bowl all at once, it can usually do the job of the flush.

Now you calculate again.

You had one hundred and eighty liters of water.

Every day you used four liters.

By the start of day seven, one hundred and fifty-six liters remain.

You still have a lot of water.

But now you can no longer count on the plumbing.

📊 Day 7 | 👥 1 Person | 💧 156 Liters | 🥫 16 Portions

**[Day 9: The Knock | 4:30]**

It's the middle of the night.

🔊 Knock... knock... knock...

Three knocks on the door.

You freeze.

From behind the door, a familiar voice comes.

"Hey! It's me! Open the door!"

It's Jax.

Your friend from the upper floor.

If you open the door, your water and food have to be split between two people.

But if you don't open it, he might really need help.

You open the door.

Jax comes in with a backpack.

Inside his bag there are a few batteries, a flashlight and a remote-control car.

He says he wants to do a test.

He wants to see if he can lure the aliens to another side using light and sound.

You look at him.

"If it works, maybe we can leave the house."

📊 Day 9 | 👥 2 People | 💧 148 Liters | 🥫 14 Portions

**[Day 12: The Remote-Control Car | 5:30]**

Jax has been with you for three days.

The food is running out faster and you both know you have to do something.

Jax has gotten the car ready.
He attaches a flashlight to it and checks the batteries.

It's two at night.

You quietly leave the house.

When you reach the entrance of the building, the light of a ship moves across the wall.

The light stops just a few meters ahead of you.

You both crouch behind the wall.

One minute.

Two minutes.

Finally the light moves away from the building.

Jax puts the car on the ground and turns it on.

The car, with the flashlight on, moves toward the street.

A few seconds later, the ship's light changes direction.

Right toward the car.

Jax whispers excitedly:

"See? I told you it would work!"

One of the aliens is also moving toward the car.

This method only distracts their attention for a short time.

But these few minutes might be enough.

📊 Day 12 | 👥 2 People | 💧 124 Liters | 🥫 8 Portions | 🚗 1 Car

**[Day 15: The Second Car | 6:30]**

Three days have passed.

Using the spare parts, you prepare a second remote-control car as well.

Now you have two devices that can distract the aliens' attention for a short time.

But there's a bigger problem.

The food is almost gone.

For two people, only two portions remain.

The water has also reached one hundred liters.

That means you still have reserves, but to keep going you have to find more food sources.

Jax points at the backpacks.

"Tomorrow morning, you're going to the supermarket."

📊 Day 15 | 👥 2 People | 💧 100 Liters | 🥫 2 Portions | 🚗 2 Cars

**[Day 16: The Supermarket | 7:00]**

It's five in the morning.

You and Jax leave the building with two backpacks.

You move slowly and stay along the walls as much as possible.

When you reach the supermarket, you're met with a terrible sight.

The shelves are almost empty.

The glass is broken and food packages are scattered on the floor.
The solution: the back storeroom.
You find untouched cartons behind the shelves.

You don't have much time.

So you only take things that are truly useful to you.

Foods that can be eaten without cooking and have a lot of energy for their weight.
Canned food.

Each backpack can't weigh more than twelve and a half kilograms.

So you choose carefully.

You collect sixteen liters of water and a lot of high-calorie food.

At ten to six you reach the building again.

You finally breathe a sigh of relief.

But just as you close the door, a strange sound comes from outside.

The sound of footsteps moving toward the building.

You both go silent.

The footsteps pass by the door.

And move away.

📊 Day 16 | 👥 2 People | 💧 108 Liters | 🥫 34 Portions | 🚗 2 Cars

**[Day 20: Watching | 7:50]**

Four days have passed.

You've covered all the windows, but you watch the street through a small gap.

For several days now you've noticed a strange pattern.

The aliens search one building completely per day,
and every building whose search is finished also gets a mark placed on its door,

and so far, you haven't seen any building be checked again.

Based on the order of their movement, there are only two days left until they reach your building.

Jax looks at you.

"So? Do we run or do we hide?"

📊 Day 20 | 👥 2 People | 💧 76 Liters | 🥫 26 Portions

**[The Decision: Run or Hide? | 8:30]**

Run?

Where?

You don't know any safe place.

In open spaces, any movement can attract the aliens' attention.

Besides, you have water and food here.

Hide?

They're searching the buildings.

If they enter your house, they'll probably find you.

So maybe a third option.

You point at the mark on the door of the neighboring building.

There's an old rule in every search: a place that has been searched once is not searched again. That's why they put that mark on the doors.

Its search finished yesterday.

And according to the pattern you saw, after marking, they move on to the next building.

"What if we go there?"

Jax looks at the building.

It's only twenty meters away.

Its door is still open.

If you can move over without drawing attention, maybe you'll survive a few more days.

Of course, there's no guarantee that the aliens won't come back.

But for now, this is your best chance.

**[Day 21: Moving | 9:20]**

Five in the morning.

It's still dark.

You turn on the first remote-control car with the flashlight and send it toward the end of the street.

The ship's light is diverted toward it for a short time.

This opportunity is enough for you.

You take the water, food, radio and blankets.

You make three trips back and forth.

Each time, only essential items.

Before six o'clock, everything has been moved to the neighboring building.

You enter an empty apartment on the third floor.

In the kitchen, you see a water heater.

The water heater's tank is itself a reserve of clean water. About forty liters.

First you cut its power and gas, then through the tap at the bottom, you drain the water into the bottles.

A water source that you hadn't thought of until now.

You close the door.

You cover the windows.

And you sit in the dark again.

Now you can only hope that the mark on the door truly means the end of the search.

📊 Day 21 | 👥 2 People | 💧 68 → +40 = 108 Liters | 🥫 24 Portions

**[Day 22: They Came | 9:50]**

On the morning of the twenty-second day, you look from behind the curtain.

Three aliens enter your previous building.

First the ground floor.

Then the first floor.

Their light passes through the windows.

Step by step they go higher.

Until one of them enters your floor.

The floor where you used to live.

A few seconds later, the ship's light falls on the window of your old home.

You realize they've entered your house.

A few minutes later, they leave the building.

And head toward the next street.

📊 Day 22 | 👥 2 People | 💧 100 Liters | 🥫 22 Portions

**[Day 30: The End | 10:10]**

Eight more days pass.

Eight days in a stranger's house.

Without light.

Without loud noise.

And with the fear that they might find you again at any moment.

But finally, the street calms down.

It's been three days since any alien has been seen in this area.

On the morning of the thirtieth day, at five o'clock, you decide to go back to your own house.
It seems the situation had gotten better and the power had come back.
You walk twenty meters.

You climb the stairs.

And you reach your front door.

But right there you freeze.

In front of the door, you see a pair of sneakers.

A pair of familiar red sneakers!

The same shoes that were left in the middle of the street on the first day.

When that girl disappeared.

You just stare for a few seconds.

Because in these thirty days, nobody except you and Jax has entered this building.

And nobody except you had seen that girl.

Jax comes quietly from behind you.

He looks at the shoes.

Then at you.

🔊 Complete silence.
